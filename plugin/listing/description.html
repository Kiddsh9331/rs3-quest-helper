RS3 Quest Buddy puts the RuneScape Wiki's quick guides inside the game, for all ~370 quests, miniquests and sagas.

  • Step card on the game screen: the current step, the chat options to pick, what to bring (checked against your backpack) and what to get.
  • The panel (Plugins ribbon): every quest with its status, the full step list, and the items you need, green when you have them. Missing items say whether they're on the Grand Exchange; switch on Ironman to see how to make them instead.
  • Highlights: the NPCs and objects the step is about get a tile outline and an arrow, including the right stairs when you're on the wrong floor.
  • Moves on by itself as you play: talking to the next person, picking up the items, climbing where the step says, arriving, or the quest's own progress. "Done, next" and the step checkboxes always work too.
  • Dialogue: the step's chat options turn blue in the conversation.
  • Finding the way: a dotted path towards the next place, a marker on the step's map tile, and which lodestone to use when it's far. Each can be switched off in Settings.

Every quest's guide comes from the wiki and was checked by script, but only a handful have been played through with the plugin so far. If a step doesn't move on, press "Done, next", and please report it (see Feedback).

Permissions

This plugin requests the optional chatAccess permission. It is used to read game messages about kills ("You have slain..."), so a step that asks you to defeat something moves on once the game says it's done. The plugin works without it; those steps then move on when the quest progresses or when you press "Done, next". No other permissions are requested.

Data protection

Any information collected does not leave the local plugin.

Data used: the plugin reads the quest statuses and progress values the game provides, your position, your backpack contents, what you click and the conversation text on screen, to know which step you're on. It saves your place in each quest and your settings. If you switch on "Recording" in Settings (off by default), it also keeps a log of your last few sessions (where you walked, your backpack, what you clicked, quest progress and conversation text; never players' chat or names) so a problem can be replayed if you choose to send it in. All of this stays in the plugin's own storage on your device. This information is not transmitted outside of the plugin under any circumstances.
